Why builders use 3D

A floor plan asks buyers to imagine. A walkthrough shows them.

Here's where a flat plan costs you time, margin, and deals.

A-01

Your buyers can't read a floor plan.

Most people can't picture 14'-0" × 16'-6" from a line drawing. So they ask for another meeting, then another.

A walkthrough answers "how big is it really?" without you in the room.

A-02

Change orders show up after framing.

Many buyers only understand the space once the walls are up. Moving an island or a wall then costs you schedule and margin.

Buyers walk it, and change it, before you order lumber.

A-03

You're up against model homes and design centers.

Production builders can afford staged models and in-house rendering teams. A boutique builder usually can't justify that hire.

Get the same polished presentation, priced per plan, with no one added to payroll.

A-04

Selections drag on for weeks.

Swatches, sample boards, and "can I see it with the darker floor?" emails slow every job down.

Buyers flip between countertop, cabinet, and floor options in the room itself.

B-01

Your buyer lives 900 miles away.

They can't drive past the lot on a Saturday or sit down with your designer.

Send one link. They tour the home from their couch, on any device.

B-02

Decisions happen over email and PDFs.

A product photo can't show how a dark counter reads next to navy cabinets in afternoon light.

They see each combination in the actual room before they commit.

B-03

Long-distance buyers hesitate to sign.

Signing for a house you've never stood in is a big ask, especially for a second home.

A furnished walkthrough is the next best thing to standing in it.

B-04

Every meeting is a screen share.

Talking someone through a plan over video is slow for both of you, and they forget half of it.

They explore on their own time and come to the call with real questions.

How it works

From plan set to shareable link in four steps.

You send plans the way you already have them. We handle the modeling, staging, and setup.

  1. Send your plans

    PDF plan sheets, CAD files, or builder brochures. Add your selections sheet if you have one.

  2. We build it to scale

    Walls, windows, doors, and ceiling heights come straight from your dimensions.

  3. Stage it and add options

    We furnish every room with real-size furniture and set up your finish options as one-tap toggles.

  4. Share one link

    Buyers open it on any phone or laptop. Put it in your listing, on your website, or in sales emails.

FAQ

Questions builders ask first.

Don't see yours? Call or email and we'll answer it directly.

What do you need from me?

Your floor plans and elevations, as PDF or CAD. If you offer finish packages, send the selections sheet too and we'll set those up as toggles.

Do buyers need to download anything?

No. The walkthrough opens in a web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. Buyers tap a link and start walking.

Can I put it on my website or listing?

Yes. You get a link you can share anywhere, plus embed code to place the walkthrough directly on your own site.

Can you model a home that hasn't been built yet?

Yes, that's what it's for. If you have a plan set, we can build the walkthrough.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the plan, how many rooms you want furnished, and how many finish options you offer. Your quote includes a delivery date.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on square footage, furnishing, and the number of finish options. Send a quote request with your plan details and we'll reply with a price.
